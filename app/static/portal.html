What does each tool do?

Identity Platform + Firestore

Check who can see the bill

Login verifies the account. The database maps that account to patients and access permissions. Mentioning another person in a question does not grant access to their records.

LangGraph + Vertex AI

Investigate the question

The agent uses two read-only tools: authorized billing records, and insurance/payment events. LangGraph organizes tool calls and evidence checks; Vertex AI supplies model inference.

UiPath Maestro + Action Center

Wait for staff, then continue

Maestro calls the agent and creates an Action Center review task. Once staff decides, Maestro resumes and returns the approved reply to the patient inbox.
